# ADORA early tester — feedback questionnaire

**Send:** after ~1 week and at end of test period.  
**Store:** [`EXTERNAL-TESTER-RECORD.md`](./EXTERNAL-TESTER-RECORD.md) (avoid unnecessary personal data).

---

## General

1. What was **immediately useful**?  
2. What was **confusing**?  
3. What felt **unnecessary**?  
4. What was **missing**?

## Workflow

5. Was the **Provider → Contract → Service → Function** relationship clear?  
6. Was the **risk** workflow useful?  
7. Was **evidence** management understandable (upload, view, remove)?  
8. Was the **relationship map** useful? For what decision?

## UX

9. Where did you **get stuck**?  
10. Which **page or label** was hardest to understand?  
11. Which **terminology** should change?

## Business value

12. What problem would ADORA **replace** (tools/processes today)?  
13. Would you use something like this **internally** if it matured?  
14. What would **prevent adoption** in your organization?  
15. What would you expect from a **production** version (hosting, SSO, integrations, support)?

## Commercial (non-leading)

16. If ADORA solved the problems you identified, would your organization **consider adopting a tool like this**? (Yes / Maybe / No — why?)  
17. Would you be open to a **longer paid pilot** with your own data/environment? (Yes / Maybe / No)

**Do not ask in this form:** “Would you pay €X?” (follow up separately if 16–17 are positive.)
