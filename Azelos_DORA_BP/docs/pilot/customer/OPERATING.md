# Operating the Blueprint (customer)

Day-to-day use of registers and oversight features. All data is **your** organization’s; reports and exports reflect what you enter.

## ICT providers

- Maintain the register of ICT third parties and material subcontractors you track in the tool.
- Use **provider detail** to see related contracts, risks, and relationship context.
- **Sub-outsourcing** (where shown) records tiers under a provider.

## Contracts

- Create contracts with reference number, dates, type, and status.
- Link each contract to exactly one provider in the standard flow.
- Update status when contracts renew or terminate.

## ICT services

- Services belong to a contract.
- Set **critical/important** classification and processing/storage locations as required by your policy.
- Open **dependencies** from a service to link business functions.

## Business functions

- Define internal functions (e.g. payments, lending channel).
- Mark **critical or important** per your internal CIF assessment.
- Link to ICT services via **Dependencies**.

## ICT assets

- Optional register of ICT assets supporting functions.
- Link assets to business functions where you model infrastructure dependencies.

## Risks

- Create assessments scoped to provider, contract, and/or service.
- Enter dimension levels; the system calculates a resulting risk level (versioned).
- Use rationale and assessor fields for oversight traceability.

## Requirements

- Each organization receives a **baseline requirement list** at setup.
- Set **applicability** and **implementation status**, owner, and notes.
- Link **evidence** where you demonstrate implementation.

## Evidence

- Choose document type, upload file; metadata is stored in the database, file bytes in configured storage.
- Download from the evidence list when authorized.
- Track expiry/review dates if your process uses them.

## Incidents

- Record ICT-related incidents with severity and status lifecycle fields.
- Link incidents to providers, services, or other entities where supported.

## Resilience testing

- Record resilience test campaigns (scenario, BCP, DR, TLPT kinds as available).
- Maintain **BCP** and **DRP** plan records linked to your resilience programme.
- Use the **Resilience** hub and **DORA overview** for aggregated counts.

## Relationship map

- Open **DORA → Relationship map** for a graph of linked entities.
- Pan and zoom; drag nodes to rearrange (**layout is session-only** until reset).
- Use the map in oversight meetings to trace **provider → service → function**.

## Reports

- Generate in-app reports (e.g. DORA/resilience assessment views available in your build).
- Reports use live database content for your tenant.

## Exports

- Export registers (e.g. ICT providers CSV) for committees or archive.
- **Tenant data export** (ZIP) for portability — handle as confidential.
