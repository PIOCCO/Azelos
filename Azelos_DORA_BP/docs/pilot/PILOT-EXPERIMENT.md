# Pilot experiment

## Hypothesis

The DORA Blueprint can **reduce operational friction** from fragmented spreadsheets and files by connecting ICT third-party, service, risk, requirement, evidence, and resilience information in **one tenant-scoped operational system**—without claiming regulatory certification.

## Questions to validate

1. Does the customer **actually have** this fragmentation / traceability problem?
2. **How do they solve it today** (Excel, GRC tool, SharePoint, CMDB, vendor manager)?
3. Which workflows are **most painful** (provider intake, contract mapping, evidence renewal, incident linkage, reporting)?
4. Which parts of the BP are **actually used** during the pilot?
5. What work **remains outside** the BP?
6. Does the **relationship** between providers, services, functions, risks, and evidence provide **practical value** in meetings or oversight?
7. Would the customer **continue using** the system after pilot?
8. Would the customer **pay** for continued use (pricing validated separately)?

**Do not assume answers**—record evidence from usage, interviews, and support tickets.
