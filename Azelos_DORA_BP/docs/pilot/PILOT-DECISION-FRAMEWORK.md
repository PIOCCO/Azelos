# Evidence-based pilot decision framework

**No scores. No manufactured pass/fail.** Collect facts under each heading; leadership decides next steps.

## Strong evidence of demand

(Facts: repeated weekly use, multiple roles, customer-led expansion of data, unprompted report/map use)

-

## Weak evidence of demand

(Facts: single user, login without data entry, abandoned after onboarding)

-

## Unresolved questions

-

## Repeated workflow problems

(Same issue across weeks/users—see weekly reviews)

-

## Product blockers

(Concrete defects reproducing in pilot—link tickets)

-

## Customer-specific requests

(One tenant only)

-

## Out-of-scope requests

(Per [PILOT-BOUNDARY.md](./PILOT-BOUNDARY.md))

-

## Evidence of willingness to continue

(Facts: ask for extension, production planning, internal budget discussion—not hypotheticals)

-

## Evidence of willingness to pay

(Facts: procurement contact, pricing conversation initiated by customer—mark pricing hypotheses separately in [COMMERCIAL-PACKAGE.md](./COMMERCIAL-PACKAGE.md))

-

## Decision options (factual triggers only)

| Observation | Possible next step |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Strong use + repeated workflow gaps | Prioritize fixes via [NEXT-DEVELOPMENT-RULE.md](./NEXT-DEVELOPMENT-RULE.md) |
| Strong use + few gaps | Production deployment planning |
| Weak use | Understand problem fit before building more |
| Blockers listed | Fix blockers first, then re-assess |
